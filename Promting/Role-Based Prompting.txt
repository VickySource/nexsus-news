## 7-Day Beginner Weight-Loss Workout Plan

This plan is designed for a beginner with a focus on:

* Burning calories
* Building consistency
* Improving strength and mobility
* Avoiding burnout or injury

### General Guidelines

* Warm up 5 minutes before every workout (marching, arm circles, light jogging).
* Cool down 5 minutes with stretching.
* Aim for 7,000–10,000 steps daily.
* Drink plenty of water.
* Keep workouts at a pace where you can still talk, but feel challenged.

---

# Day 1 — Full Body Beginner Strength + Walk

### Workout (2 rounds)

* Bodyweight Squats — 12 reps
* Wall Push-Ups — 10 reps
* Glute Bridges — 15 reps
* Standing Knee Raises — 20 total
* Plank — 20 seconds

Rest 60 seconds between rounds.

### Cardio

* 20-minute brisk walk

---

# Day 2 — Low-Impact Cardio

### 30–40 Minutes

Choose one:

* Fast walking
* Cycling
* Elliptical
* Dancing workout

Keep intensity moderate.

### Core Finisher

* Dead Bugs — 10 each side
* Bird Dogs — 10 each side
* Side Plank — 15 seconds each side

Repeat 2 rounds.

---

# Day 3 — Lower Body + Core

### Workout (3 rounds)

* Squats — 12 reps
* Reverse Lunges — 8 each leg
* Glute Bridges — 15 reps
* Calf Raises — 15 reps
* Mountain Climbers — 20 total

### Optional

* 15-minute easy walk after workout

---

# Day 4 — Active Recovery

### Recovery Focus

* 30-minute relaxed walk
* Gentle stretching or beginner yoga

Focus on mobility and recovery.

---

# Day 5 — Upper Body + Cardio

### Workout (3 rounds)

* Incline Push-Ups (bench/table) — 10 reps
* Dumbbell or Water Bottle Rows — 12 reps
* Shoulder Press — 12 reps
* Chair Dips — 8 reps
* Plank — 25 seconds

### Cardio

* 15–20 minutes brisk walking

---

# Day 6 — Fat-Burning Circuit

### Circuit (3 rounds)

Perform each exercise for 30 seconds:

* Marching in Place
* Squats
* Step-Ups
* Arm Punches
* Modified Jumping Jacks
* High Knees (low impact)

Rest 1 minute between rounds.

---

# Day 7 — Recovery + Light Activity

### Choose One

* Easy walk
* Light cycling
* Stretching session
* Yoga

### Goal

Move gently for 20–30 minutes.

---

# Beginner Progression Tips

After 2–3 weeks:

* Add one extra round
* Increase walking speed
* Extend planks by 10 seconds
* Add light dumbbells/resistance bands

---

# Simple Weight-Loss Nutrition Tips

* Eat protein with every meal
* Reduce sugary drinks
* Fill half your plate with vegetables
* Avoid extreme dieting
* Sleep 7–8 hours nightly
